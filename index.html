Ceremonies

A Week of Traditions, Leading to the Wedding

1
Formal Agreement

Sandhu

The groom presents "Aaku Vokka" (betel leaves) to our uncle — a customary gesture sealing the two families' blessing on the wedding.

2
At the Bride's Relatives' Home

Nalugu (Bride)

A turmeric ceremony for Deepthi, celebrated with our relatives ahead of the big day.

3
The Evening Before

Tharalipovadam

With joy and celebration, we escort Deepthi in procession from our home to the groom's home.

4
Morning of the Wedding

Inti Nalugu

A turmeric ceremony for both Deepthi and Ravi together, held at the groom's home.
